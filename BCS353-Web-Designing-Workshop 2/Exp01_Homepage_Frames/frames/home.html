Welcome to BookHaven

BookHaven is an online bookstore website designed for the students and faculty of GL Bajaj Institute of Technology & Management. It brings the entire departmental book catalogue into one easy-to-use portal.

Using this website you can:

  • Browse department-wise catalogues (CSE, ECE, EEE and CIVIL) using the links in the left frame.
  • View book cover, author, publisher and price in the Catalogue page.
  • Add books to your Cart and review the total amount.
  • Create an account from the Registration page and sign in from the Login page.

How the three frames work

  • Top frame: logo, college name and main navigation (Home, Login, Registration, Catalogue, Cart).
  • Left frame: department links CSE / ECE / EEE / CIVIL.
  • Right frame: all linked pages load here. For example, clicking “CSE” displays the CSE book catalogue in this frame.

Tip: use the navigation bar above or the department list on the left to move around the site.
